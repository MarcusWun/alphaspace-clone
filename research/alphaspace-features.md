# AlphaSpace Feature Research
_Sources: Fortune exclusive interview (May 19 2026), Yahoo Inc press release, Yahoo Finance article_

## What AlphaSpace Is
- Launched May 19 2026 by Yahoo Finance
- Positioned as "Bloomberg Terminal for retail investors"
- Bloomberg Terminal = ~$27,000/yr; AlphaSpace = $39.99/mo (Yahoo Finance Gold)
- 7-day free trial available
- Pure research platform — NO brokerage, no buy buttons

## Core Concept
- **Financial canvas** — blank workspace, drag-and-drop panels
- Panels can be **linked**: clicking a ticker in watchlist updates chart + news feed simultaneously
- Fully **persistent** across sessions (unlike tab-based Yahoo Finance)
- Pre-built **themes** (e.g., Magnificent Seven view)
- AI can generate views on command: "compare Microsoft and Google" → side-by-side panel

## Feature List (confirmed from sources)

### Canvas / Layout
- Drag-and-drop panel system
- Resize panels
- Save/restore workspace layouts
- Cross-session persistence
- Pre-defined layout themes
- Context-driven navigation, modular/adaptable

### Charting
- Advanced charts (implied: candlestick, OHLC, line)
- User-defined time periods
- **Multi-stock group comparison** (normalize to % change, overlay lines) — Marcus's favorite feature
- Technical analysis tools (Yahoo Finance Gold includes 50 chart patterns)

### Data & Fundamentals
- Real-time (or near-real-time) quotes
- Fundamental data — financials, estimates, 40 years of company data
- Portfolio tracking panel
- Alerts
- Watchlist panels
- Peer group comparison
- SEC filing viewer

### News
- Written + video news from Yahoo Finance and partners
- Ticker-linked news feeds (filter by portfolio, individual stock, sector)
- **Dockable video** (picture-in-picture while researching)
- Live and on-demand video

### AI (Yahoo Scout)
- Embedded across all workflows
- Summarizes market developments
- Builds and modifies workspace views via natural language
- Answers investor questions in-platform
- Adds comparative charts on command

### Portfolio
- Portfolio view panel
- No trading — research only
- Portfolio-linked news feeds

## What's NOT in AlphaSpace
- No trade execution / brokerage
- No portfolio rebalancing
- No mobile app mentioned (web-first)

## Pricing
- $39.99/month as part of Yahoo Finance Gold
- Annual: $479.40/yr
- 7-day free trial
